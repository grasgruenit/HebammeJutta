### A place to connect, share &amp; ask questions

You’ve become a mum – and suddenly, everything is new.  
As beautiful as those first weeks and months with your baby can be, they can
also bring lots of questions, uncertainty and new challenges. At times, it can
feel overwhelming or even a little lonely. Meeting and talking to other parents
can be a wonderful reminder that many of the worries, questions and little
challenges we face are completely normal.
The Baby Café is a welcoming space where you can meet other mums and
dads, have a chat and share your experiences. Whether it’s breastfeeding,
introducing solids, sleep, your baby’s development, parenting or
everyday family life – everything is welcome. Older siblings are very
welcome too, and there is plenty of space for them to play.
